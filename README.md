# Hi, I’m David 👋

What happens if we add one more loading dock? That’s the kind of question I like turning into a model.

I study mechanical engineering at Lund University, specialising in logistics and production economics. I’ve worked in air cargo at Copenhagen Airport, and I’m curious about how AI works and where it can help businesses make better decisions.

📦 I started **Demand2Decision** to explore how demand forecasts can inform inventory decisions. Once I got into the data, I switched to a simpler bike rental dataset to learn the fundamentals of training, tuning and testing models. I want to bring what I learned back to Demand2Decision.

🚲 [Bike Rental Demand Modelling](https://github.com/davidalb01/bike-rental-modelling) — I compared four models to see how well weather and calendar data predict daily rentals.
